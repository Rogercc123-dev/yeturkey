vision

YE is widely regarded as one of the most influential artists of the past two decades. His work has shaped music, fashion, design and global culture. His live performances are rare and known for their bold artistic vision and immersive scale.

This will not be a standard concert. The globe stage returns — a rotating sphere designed with Aus Taylor, YE performing masked atop a world that turns beneath him. The crowd surrounds 360 degrees. Fog. Live-corrected lights. Nothing automated.

This marks his first performance in Turkey and one of the largest stadium productions the country has ever seen. An experience designed for presence.
