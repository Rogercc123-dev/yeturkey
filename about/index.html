Introduction

Founded in 2019 by John S. Burton, Access Opera is a global production agency connecting the discipline and scale of classical music with popular music, entertainment and contemporary culture.

02

The Access Opera Story

Built between worlds.

Access Opera began with a simple conviction: classical voices belong inside contemporary culture, not at its edge. Burton’s work as an impresario, producer, curator and talent manager gave the company a point of view across both worlds.

What began in opera expanded naturally into concerts and touring, artist development, musical casting and major live productions. The through-line remains the same: assemble the right talent and make ambitious ideas real.
